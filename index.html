How I work

A simple process that keeps decisions tied to users, not opinions.

  1. ResearchTalk to users, review competitors, define the real problem.
  2. WireframeGrey boxes first. Fix structure before spending time on style.
  3. PrototypeClickable flows to test with users and align stakeholders.
  4. Hand offOrganised Figma files and specs, so developers can build with confidence.

Quick answers

Things clients often ask, and how I handle them.

“Can you make the logo bigger?”

Yes. First we’ll look at what the logo needs to do on that screen, then size it to match.

“Can we add one more feature?”

Usually, once we’ve checked it solves a real user problem and fits the timeline.

“Make it pop.”

I’ll ask what should stand out and why, then use contrast, hierarchy or motion to get it there.

“How fast can you start?”

Send a short brief and I’ll reply with a timeline and next steps.

Have a product that needs better UX?

Send me a short brief or a link to what you have now. I’ll reply with questions and a rough plan.
